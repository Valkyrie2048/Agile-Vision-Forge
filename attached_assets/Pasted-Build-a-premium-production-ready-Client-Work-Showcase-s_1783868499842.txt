Primary Objective

Avoid a generic portfolio template. Each project should feel like a meaningful product story.

* A premium portfolio landing page

Project-Specific Content

GIGAMATIC Finance

Positioning statement:
A financial platform designed around the realities of independent and nontraditional work.

Product vision:
Help gig workers, freelancers, contractors, and people with multiple sources of income understand their finances and access more appropriate financial products.

Potential capabilities:

* Consolidated income visibility
* Connected bank and employment information
* Income trends and financial insights
* Financial-product discovery
* Personalized recommendations
* Financial-readiness indicators
* Lending and mortgage pathways
* User-controlled financial data

Design considerations:

* Financial trust
* Clear explanations
* Data transparency
* Complex income patterns
* Mobile-first accessibility
* Avoiding judgmental financial language

Clearly describe future or conceptual functionality as product vision where it is not currently operational.

GIGAMATIC Insure

Positioning statement:
Insurance and benefits designed for people whose careers do not follow a traditional employment model.

Product vision:
Make it easier for independent workers to discover and understand insurance, health benefits, income protection, and related services.

Potential capabilities:

* Personalized insurance discovery
* Coverage-category exploration
* Simplified policy explanations
* Health and wellness benefits
* Income-protection options
* Life and disability insurance pathways
* Comparison and recommendation experiences
* Connections to relevant providers

Design considerations:

* Reducing insurance complexity
* Clear eligibility communication
* Trust and privacy
* Plain-language explanations
* Supporting informed decisions without presenting the platform as a licensed insurance advisor

GIGAMATIC Careers

Positioning statement:
A career platform built for modern, flexible, and multidimensional working lives.

Product vision:
Help people understand their work history, transferable capabilities, income opportunities, and potential career paths across traditional employment, contract work, and the gig economy.

Potential capabilities:

* Career profile
* Skills and experience mapping
* Transferable-skills discovery
* Personalized career pathways
* Job and contract opportunity matching
* Income-opportunity exploration
* AI-supported career guidance
* Learning and development recommendations
* Portfolio and work-history support

Design considerations:

* Inclusive career language
* Multiple employment types
* Skills beyond traditional job titles
* Explainable recommendations
* User agency and control
* Avoiding deterministic AI career predictions

GIGAMATIC AI

Positioning statement:
An intelligent layer connecting work, income, financial health, protection, and opportunity.

Present GIGAMATIC AI as the broader intelligence and product ecosystem behind the GIGAMATIC platform.

Potential capabilities:

* Personalized insights across GIGAMATIC products
* Conversational product navigation
* Intelligent recommendations
* Cross-platform user context
* Goal-oriented financial and career guidance
* Agent-assisted workflows
* Explainable recommendation logic
* User-controlled memory and permissions
* Proactive identification of opportunities or risks

Design considerations:

* Explainability
* Consent
* Privacy
* User control
* Responsible automation
* Clear distinction between guidance and regulated professional advice

The Institute for AI and the Future of Consciousness

Positioning statement:
A research and public-interest initiative exploring how artificial intelligence may shape consciousness, human agency, flourishing, society, and the future of intelligent life.

Product vision:
Create an intellectually credible yet accessible digital platform for research, publishing, public dialogue, collaboration, and responsible inquiry.

Potential capabilities:

* Research articles
* Essays and opinion pieces
* Research-library organization
* Topic and theme navigation
* Contributor profiles
* Public events and initiatives
* Newsletter and community engagement
* Calls for participation
* Partnership and speaking information
* Educational resources
* Responsible AI and human-flourishing content

Design considerations:

* Academic credibility without institutional coldness
* Serious editorial presentation
* Clear hierarchy for long-form writing
* Accessibility and readability
* Philosophical depth
* Human-centred visual language

An agentic iOS travel companion designed to help people explore, plan, navigate, and adapt throughout a journey.

Move beyond static itinerary planning by creating an intelligent travel assistant that understands context, location, preferences, timing, and changing circumstances.

Potential capabilities:

* Conversational trip planning
* Personalized destination discovery

* Real-time itinerary adaptation
* Location-aware recommendations
* Flight, hotel, and activity research
* Calendar and reservation awareness
* Navigation assistance
* Voice interaction
* Proactive travel alerts
* Collaborative trip planning
* Preference and memory controls
* Human approval before bookings or consequential actions

Present the product as an iOS application.

Include iPhone mockups throughout the case study.

Design considerations:

* Native iOS interaction patterns
* Voice and touch interaction
