---
name: pnpm audit override safety
description: Prevent generated audit overrides from selecting incompatible majors or lower vulnerable patches.
---

Treat overrides generated by `pnpm audit --fix` as a starting point, not a safe final configuration. Replace open-ended replacement ranges with explicit patched versions, and make overlapping selectors resolve to the highest required patch.

**Why:** Open-ended replacements selected incompatible major releases in transitive code-generation tooling, while overlapping exact replacements allowed a narrower rule to reintroduce a vulnerable lower patch.

**How to apply:** After any pnpm audit fix, inspect every new override, regenerate the lockfile, run the affected transitive tools (not only the main build), and rerun the audit at low severity.
